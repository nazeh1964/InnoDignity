InnoDignity

Restoring Human Dignity Through Sovereign Bio-Intelligence

About the Project

InnoDignity is a vertically integrated deep-tech ecosystem designed to restore personal autonomy, self-confidence, and dignity for the elderly, disabled, and neonatal care. Founded and led by Nazeh Osman from Tamra, Israel, the initiative bridges precision engineering with offline Sovereign AI.

Core Technological Pillars

  • Smart Bio-Garments: Dry-electrode medical textiles and advanced bio-sensing fabrics, including specialized medical underwear (תחתונים), for continuous health monitoring.
  • Sovereign Digital Secretary (SDS): On-device, localized multilingual AI companion ensuring complete data privacy without cloud dependencies.
  • Sovereign Bed & Autonomy Technologies: Mechatronic dynamics allowing seamless transition from sleeping to sitting and standing positions, combined with automated care protocols.

The Nine Zeros / Five Zeros Doctrine

Our design philosophy revolves around absolute sustainability, safety, and operational perfection:

  • Zero waste, zero pollution, and zero harm.
  • Zero odor, zero clinical error, and zero neglect.
  • Zero exclusion, zero data-loss, and zero indignity.
